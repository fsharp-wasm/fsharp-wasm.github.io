_Last updated_: April 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how `https://fsharp-wasm.github.io` (the "Site") handles information when you visit the site.

## What we collect

**This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising networks, or sell user data.**

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service:

- **Server logs**: Standard web server logs (IP address, browser, page visited, timestamp). These are retained for security and debugging purposes.
- **GitHub Analytics**: If you interact with GitHub repositories linked from this site (issues, discussions, stars), GitHub's own privacy policy applies.

## What we do not collect

- We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics by default
- We do not set advertising cookies
- We do not track you across sites
- We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily submit it (e.g. opening a GitHub issue)

## RSS feed

Our RSS feed (`/rss.xml`) is a public feed. Subscribing to it via a feed reader is anonymous from our perspective.

## Third-party services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's privacy policy applies to the hosting infrastructure: [https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Embedded content

Blog posts and documentation may link to external sites (GitHub, documentation, etc.). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Changes

We will update this policy as the site evolves. The last updated date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

## Contact

For privacy concerns, open a [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/fsharp-wasm/fsharp-wasm/discussions) or an issue in the [site repository](https://github.com/fsharp-wasm/fsharp-wasm.github.io).
